pair your tablet

WI-FI recommended

Connect PC and tablet to the same network. No USB required after setup. Leave the address blank to discover one tablet automatically.

USB CABLE optional setup

Plug the tablet in. The app finds it through adb — a ROM update (~340 MB) moves in about a minute. Best path for big files.

BLUETOOTH wireless

Open “Connect / pair new PC” on the tablet. Find it below and confirm the matching numbers on both screens. Remembered PCs reconnect without another code.

tip: the tablet turns Bluetooth on by itself during setup. After setup: Settings > Bluetooth.
